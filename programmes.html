Our programmes

From the soil to the shelf

Six connected programmes take farmers from raw production to market-ready, high-value products, raising incomes and competitiveness.

  • Organic Production
  • Farmer Capacity Building
  • Essential Oils
  • Community Nutrition
  • Market Access
  • Research
Programme 01

Organic Production & Climate-Smart Agriculture

We train farmers in organic and climate-smart practices, promoting diversified cropping, soil regeneration and water-efficient farming to build resilient livelihoods.

More than 800 farmers have been trained in the art and science of climate-smart farming, through partnerships with organisations such as Hakyoogo Village Nature Services, which leads demonstrations of these innovations.

800+farmers trained in climate-smart farming
100%organic, chemical-free practices
  • Diversified cropping systems
  • Composting & soil regeneration
  • Water-efficient farming
  • Resilience to drought and floods
Programme 02

Farmer Capacity Building

We run extensive training in agronomy, post-harvest handling, organic certification, entrepreneurship and market-led production to professionalise smallholder farmers.

Over 1,500 farmers in Western Uganda have benefited so far, reached through radio programmes, community mobilisation, selected home visits and online engagement.

1,500+farmers trained
4outreach channels
  • Radio programmes
  • Community mobilisation
  • Selected home visits
  • Online engagement
Programme 03

Essential Oils & Value Addition

Through our business and processing arm, TOPA supports the processing and value addition of high-value organic crops, so farmers move from raw produce to market-ready products.

Value addition raises incomes and competitiveness and keeps more of the value chain in Tooro communities.

4core value chains
9+finished products
  • Chia seeds & chia oil
  • Castor seeds & castor oil
  • Rosemary, mint & other essential oils
  • Herbal products & tonics
Programme 04

Community Nutrition & Health

Our organic nutrition products and herbal remedies support household nutrition, immune health and the management of chronic conditions, contributing to community wellbeing.

We have recorded more than 500 testimonies from members on the value of these trainings and the health improvements they have experienced.

500+member testimonies
1new focus: child cognition
  • Household nutrition
  • Immune health
  • Natural support for chronic conditions
  • Chia-enriched diets for children
Programme 05

Market Access & Export Readiness

We build links with domestic distributors and international buyers, positioning Ugandan organic products for European and Middle Eastern markets.

Our products are gaining traction: samples went to Europe and the Americas in 2025, and we are now focusing on Asia, specifically the United Arab Emirates.

3continents reached with samples
2025first export samples
  • Domestic distribution via Fortiemart Market Place
  • Europe & the Americas
  • UAE & Middle East focus
  • Certification & export scaling
Programme 06

Research, Evidence & Farmers Hub

Our Farmers Hub brings together farmers, researchers and volunteers to co-create knowledge and test innovations in real fields.

Demonstration gardens, learning visits and field days turn evidence into practice and help the best ideas spread from farmer to farmer.

9districts of learning
∞ideas from farmers
  • Demonstration gardens
  • Learning visits & field days
  • Research partnerships
  • Knowledge co-creation

Together, we can build Africa’s organic economy.

Farmers, investors, donors, researchers and buyers: there is a place for you in Uganda’s organic transformation, including our new Chia for Brain Capital initiative.
