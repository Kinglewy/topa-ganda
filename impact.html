Impact & results

Measurable change for people and planet

Economic, social and environmental results from a decade of farmer-led organic agriculture in Western Uganda.

1,500+Farmers in structured organic value chains
800+Trained in climate-smart farming
500+Recorded health testimonies
9Districts reached

Three dimensions of impact

Healthier families, stronger incomes, regenerated soils

Economic impact

  • Over 1,500 farmers integrated into structured organic value chains
  • Millions invested in rural economies through farmer payments and operations
  • Value addition that raises farm-gate incomes

New target: 15,000 chia farmers trained by 2035

Social & health impact

  • Improved community nutrition and immune health
  • Natural support for managing chronic conditions
  • An inclusive membership: 57% women, 40% youth, 3% PWDs

New focus: cognitive development in children through chia-enriched diets

Environmental impact

  • Organic farming that cuts chemical pollution
  • Soil regeneration through composting and agroecological practices
  • Climate-smart systems that build resilience to drought and floods

Vision 2030: 10,000 empowered organic farmers
